Privacy

You can play every game without signing in. Then nothing leaves your device: progress is saved only in this browser.

If a parent signs in

  • Who signs in: a parent, with their own Google account. Children never sign in and never type anything about themselves.
  • What we keep: the parent's Google sign-in (handled by Google Firebase), the nicknames the parent types for each child, and each child's game progress (levels, stars, answers inside the games).
  • What we never ask for: a child's real name, age, school, photo or location. Please use a nickname.
  • Who can see it: only the signed-in parent. It is not shared or sold, and there are no ads.
  • Where it is stored: Google Firebase (Firestore), so the progress follows the child to other devices.

Deleting

  • Remove a child: on the home page, choose "Remove a child". Their progress is deleted everywhere.
  • Delete everything: choose "Delete account and all data". The account, all children and all progress are deleted.
  • Sign out: takes the children's progress off that device; it stays in the account for the next sign-in.
